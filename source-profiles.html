SPECIES WP 3

Name: Benjamin Dupuis

Position: Post-doc at Department of Ecoscience, Aarhus University, Denmark

Profile: I am working in movement ecology and population biology, focusing on how environmental variation and human activities influence wildlife. I'm particularly interested in understanding how individual physiological and behavioral responses to stressors scale up to affect population dynamics. To do so, I combine empirical data, bio-logging and agent-based modelling.

Name: Floris M. van Beest

Position: Senior Researcher at Department of Ecoscience, Aarhus University, Denmark

Profile: I am an animal ecologist with a scientific focus on movement ecology, population biology, climate change, and ecological modelling. Central themes in my research include understanding the role of environmental variation and anthropogenic stressors on movement behaviour and fitness of animals (on land and in the sea) and the consequences for population dynamics. Throughout my research projects, I combine empirical data, statistical analyses, and agent-based modelling.
