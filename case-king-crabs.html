Case studies/King crabs
Norway · Sápmi

King crabs.

Fisheries, livelihoods and global markets

Following the connections between Arctic fisheries, marine ecosystems, local livelihoods and international markets.

King crab fishing has become a lucrative industry in Northern Norway. Its expansion also raises questions about ecological change, seabed impacts and the future of local livelihoods.

This case brings local and Indigenous fishers, marine biologists and anthropologists into dialogue about the relationship between fishing, community livelihoods and sustainable marine ecosystems.

The research also follows live king crabs from northern Norway to restaurants and hotels in China. QR codes accompanying exported crabs connect consumers to stories about fishers, their families and claims to sustainability, opening questions about how Arctic marine life becomes entangled in global markets.

How we work

Collaborative research with fishers, marine biology and anthropological work following international trade connections.
